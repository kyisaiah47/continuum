## Core Value Proposition

**Traditional CRM Problem:**
Companies hoard customer data. Customers have no control. Privacy breaches everywhere. Same data duplicated across hundreds of companies.

**Our Solution:**
Flip the model. Customers own their data in encrypted wallets. Businesses pay for temporary access. Everyone wins.

### How It Works
**For Businesses:**
1. Request access to customer data (email, purchase history, etc.)
2. Offer payment in DOT for X days
3. Customer approves → you get access
4. Access auto-expires, customer can revoke anytime

**For Customers:**
1. Create profile in Polkadot wallet
2. Businesses request your data
3. Approve → get paid in DOT
4. Control who sees what, for how long
